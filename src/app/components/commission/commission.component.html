The Commission
Process

Commission yours. At KROHN, every surface is your choice and every decision is yours to make. The body in metal or colour. The backplate in a material of your choosing. The buttons and camera rings curated to complete the composition.

We do not offer configurations. We offer conversations — and from those conversations, we build something that has never existed before.

"We simply provide the canvas worthy of your masterpiece."

The KROHN Standard
01
Consultation

A private appointment to discuss your vision. Materials, finishes, and configurations are explored — with no detail too small, and no standard too high.

02
Selection

Your choices are presented in hand. Swatches curated, combinations considered, configuration confirmed. Nothing proceeds until it is exactly right.

03
Crafting

Your piece enters the atelier. Hand-assembled by our artisans using precision instruments, each element fitted and finished to the standard your commission demands.

04
Delivery

Your completed piece arrives in a KROHN heirloom presentation box, delivered white-glove to you. The beginning of something that will outlast the ordinary.
